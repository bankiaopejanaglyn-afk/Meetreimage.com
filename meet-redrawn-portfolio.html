Process

How the redesign came together.

Four passes, in order — each one narrows the identity down until only the essential parts of Meet are left.

01 / Teardown

Study the original

Break down Meet's existing mark, UI, and motion to see which parts actually carry the brand — and which are just decoration.

02 / Palette & type

Cut to two colors

Replace the four-color mark with a black-and-dark-red duotone. Pair Space Grotesk with Inter for a tighter, more editorial voice.

03 / Motion system

Design the feel

Build the moments: frosted glass panels, a cursor-reactive glow, and a linear parallax that moves with the scroll, not against it.

04 / Assembly

Rebuild the product

Reconstruct the interface end to end — nav, call screen, controls — until the concept reads like a real, shippable product.
